# Budget Tracking Template

## Overview

This budget tracking template provides a comprehensive framework for planning, monitoring, and analyzing budgets across departments or projects. It combines automated calculations with visual dashboards to enable effective financial management and decision-making.

- Forecast vs. actual comparison

**Department Budget Tabs**:
- Individual department budget tracking
- Monthly expense categorization
- Quarterly performance reviews
- Annual budget consolidation

**Variance Analysis Tab**:
- Automated variance calculations
- Favorable vs. unfavorable categorization
- Trend analysis of budget performance
- Exception reporting for significant variances

**Forecasting Tab**:
- Rolling 12-month forecasts
- Scenario analysis capabilities
- Seasonal adjustment factors
- Accuracy tracking of predictions

**Budget Metrics**:
- Original budget allocations

## Analysis & Reporting

### Variance Analysis Framework

**Variance Calculation**:
- Formula: `Actual - Budget`
- Positive variance: Over budget (unfavorable)
- Negative variance: Under budget (favorable)
- Percentage variance: `(Actual - Budget) / Budget`

**Significance Testing**:
- Material variance: >5% or >$10,000
- Investigate all variances >10%
- Track variance patterns over time
- Identify one-time vs. recurring variances

**Variance Categories**:
- **Price Variances**: Changes in unit costs
- **Volume Variances**: Changes in quantity used
- **Efficiency Variances**: Changes in resource productivity
- **Timing Variances**: Differences in expense timing
