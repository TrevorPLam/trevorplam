# People, HR & Compliance Management

Building high-performing organizations requires both strategic people leadership and rigorous compliance management. I focus on creating systems that attract top talent, develop existing teams, and ensure organizational compliance with all applicable regulations.

## Core Competencies

### Full-Cycle Recruiting & Onboarding
I manage the complete employee lifecycle from sourcing to productive team member:

- **Talent Acquisition**: Developing sourcing strategies that reach diverse, qualified candidates
- **Interview & Selection**: Implementing structured interview processes that predict success
- **Onboarding Systems**: Creating comprehensive 90-day onboarding programs that accelerate time-to-productivity
- **Background Screening**: Managing thorough screening processes while ensuring compliance
- **Offer Management**: Handling compensation negotiations and offer communications

### Benefits Administration & Compliance
Managing employee benefits requires both compassion and precision:

- **Benefits Management**: Administering medical, dental, vision, and retirement plans
- **ERISA & ACA Compliance**: Ensuring adherence to federal regulations and reporting requirements
- **Leave Management**: Implementing FMLA, ADA, and state-specific leave policies
- **Open Enrollment**: Managing annual benefits enrollment and education programs
- **Vendor Relations**: Managing relationships with benefits providers and insurance carriers

### Employee Relations & Performance Management
Creating positive, productive work environments while managing performance:

- **Performance Management Systems**: Developing structured review processes and improvement plans
- **Employee Relations**: Handling difficult conversations, investigations, and conflict resolution
- **Policy Development**: Creating and maintaining comprehensive employee handbooks and policies
- **Engagement Initiatives**: Developing programs that improve employee satisfaction and retention
- **Exit Management**: Managing offboarding processes and knowledge transfer

### Payroll & Compensation Management
Ensuring accurate, timely compensation while maintaining compliance:

- **Payroll Processing**: Managing multi-state payroll for 300+ employees across multiple entities
- **Compensation Structures**: Developing salary bands and bonus structures that are competitive and equitable
- **Tax Compliance**: Managing multi-state tax compliance, reporting, and audits
- **Time & Attendance**: Implementing systems that accurately track hours and manage overtime
- **Record Management**: Maintaining accurate employee records and reporting systems

## Strategic Leadership

### Organizational Development
Building organizational capability through structured development programs:

- **Leadership Training**: Developing management capabilities and leadership pipelines
- **Skills Assessment**: Creating competency frameworks and development plans
- **Succession Planning**: Identifying and developing future leaders within the organization
- **Change Management**: Leading organizational change while maintaining productivity and morale
- **Culture Development**: Creating and maintaining positive, productive organizational cultures

### Risk Management & Compliance
Protecting the organization through proactive risk management:

- **Compliance Audits**: Conducting regular internal audits and external assessments
- **Policy Updates**: Maintaining current policies in response to regulatory changes
- **Risk Assessment**: Identifying and mitigating employment law and regulatory risks
- **Documentation Management**: Creating and maintaining comprehensive compliance documentation
- **Training Programs**: Developing ongoing compliance education for all employees

## Measurable Impact

My people and HR leadership consistently delivers:

- **Reduced Turnover**: 25% improvement in retention through targeted engagement and development programs
- **Faster Hiring**: 40% reduction in time-to-fill through improved sourcing and selection processes
- **Compliance Excellence**: 99%+ compliance scores in internal and external audits
- **Employee Development**: 85% internal promotion rate through development and succession planning

This integrated approach to people management ensures organizations can attract, develop, and retain top talent while maintaining full compliance with all applicable regulations and creating environments where people can do their best work.
