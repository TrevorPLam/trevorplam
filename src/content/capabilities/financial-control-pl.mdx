# Financial Control & P&L Management

Effective financial management requires both technical accounting expertise and strategic business thinking. I specialize in building financial systems that provide accurate reporting, ensure compliance, and support business decision-making.

## Core Competencies

### Multi-Entity Accounting & Bookkeeping
Managing complex financial structures across multiple business entities:

- **General Ledger Management**: Maintaining accurate chart of accounts and transaction recording
- **Multi-Entity Consolidation**: Combining financial results across related entities for reporting
- **Account Reconciliation**: Ensuring zero discrepancies across bank, credit card, and vendor accounts
- **Journal Entry Management**: Creating and reviewing journal entries with proper supporting documentation
- **Fixed Asset Management**: Tracking depreciation, disposals, and capital expenditures

### Payroll Processing & Compliance
Managing payroll for 300+ employees across multiple entities:

- **Multi-State Payroll**: Processing payroll while complying with varying state regulations
- **Tax Compliance**: Managing payroll taxes, unemployment, and workers' compensation requirements
- **Benefits Administration**: Coordinating payroll deductions for health insurance, retirement plans, and other benefits
- **Time Management**: Integrating time tracking systems with payroll processing
- **Year-End Reporting**: Generating W-2s, 1099s, and annual payroll tax filings

### Financial Reporting & Analysis
Providing timely, accurate financial information for decision-making:

- **Month-End Close**: Leading efficient closing processes that deliver results in 2-3 days
- **Financial Statement Preparation**: Creating P&L, balance sheet, and cash flow statements
- **Variance Analysis**: Investigating and explaining differences between budget and actual results
- **KPI Dashboard Development**: Building real-time visibility into key financial metrics
- **Management Reporting**: Creating tailored reports for different stakeholder needs

### Accounts Payable & Receivable Management
Managing cash flow and vendor relationships:

- **AP Processing**: Managing invoice processing, approval workflows, and payment timing
- **Collections Management**: Implementing systematic collection processes and aging reports
- **Cash Flow Management**: Optimizing payment timing and collection strategies
- **Vendor Relations**: Maintaining positive vendor relationships while negotiating favorable terms
- **Credit Management**: Setting and enforcing credit policies for customers

## Systems & Technology

### Accounting Systems Implementation
Leveraging technology to improve financial processes:

- **QuickBooks Online & Xero**: Implementing and managing cloud accounting platforms
- **Process Automation**: Using technology to reduce manual data entry and improve accuracy
- **Integration Management**: Connecting accounting systems with POS, inventory, and CRM platforms
- **Data Analytics**: Using financial data to identify trends and improvement opportunities
- **Security & Controls**: Implementing segregation of duties and access controls

### Budget & Forecast Management
Planning and monitoring financial performance:

- **Budget Development**: Creating annual budgets and periodic forecasts
- **Variance Analysis**: Comparing actual results to budget and investigating significant differences
- **Cash Flow Forecasting**: Projecting future cash needs and managing working capital
- **Capital Budgeting**: Planning and tracking capital expenditures and ROI analysis
- **Performance Monitoring**: Creating dashboards and alerts for key financial metrics

## Compliance & Risk Management

### Financial Controls & Compliance
Ensuring accuracy and regulatory compliance:

- **Internal Controls**: Developing and maintaining systems to prevent errors and fraud
- **Audit Preparation**: Managing internal and external audit processes
- **Tax Compliance**: Ensuring compliance with federal, state, and local tax requirements
- **Documentation Management**: Maintaining comprehensive financial policies and procedures
- **Risk Assessment**: Identifying and mitigating financial risks

## Measurable Impact

My financial management consistently delivers:

- **Faster Closing**: 60% reduction in month-end close time through process optimization
- **Improved Accuracy**: 99%+ reconciliation accuracy through systematic processes and automation
- **Cost Savings**: 10-15% reduction in controllable expenses through better visibility and control
- **Better Decision-Making**: Real-time financial information enabling faster, more informed decisions

This comprehensive approach to financial control ensures businesses have accurate, timely information while maintaining compliance and implementing systems that scale with growth.
