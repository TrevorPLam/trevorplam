# Grandlux Restaurant Group: Financial Operations Transformation

## The Situation

Grandlux Restaurant Group, a multi-location fine dining establishment, was struggling with inefficient financial operations. Monthly financial closes were taking 12 days, and inventory accuracy was only 76%, resulting in poor business decisions and food cost control problems.

## What Was Inherited

- **Financial Close Time**: 12 days average
- **Inventory Accuracy**: 76% (24% variance)
- **Food Cost Control**: Poor visibility into actual costs
- **Decision Making**: Based on inaccurate data
- **Multi-Location Complexity**: No standardized processes
- **Manual Reporting**: Labor-intensive consolidation

## The Methodology

### Phase 1: Financial Process Analysis
Conducted comprehensive review of financial operations:
- Month-end close procedures
- Inventory counting and reconciliation
- Food cost calculation methods
- Multi-location reporting consolidation
- Decision support systems

### Phase 2: System Redesign
Implemented standardized financial framework:
- Automated month-end close checklist
- Digital inventory management system
- Standardized food cost tracking
- Centralized reporting dashboard
- Cross-location standardization

### Phase 3: Process Implementation
Rolled out new systems across all locations:
- Staff training on new processes
- Progressive implementation with feedback
- Quality assurance metrics
- Continuous improvement monitoring

## The Outcome

### Quantitative Results
- **Financial Close Time**: Reduced from 12 days to 4 days (67% improvement)
- **Inventory Accuracy**: Improved from 76% to 94.2%
- **Food Cost Variance**: Reduced by 75%
- **Reporting Efficiency**: 80% reduction in manual work

### Qualitative Impact
- Enhanced decision-making capabilities
- Improved food cost control
- Better visibility into operations
- Scalable processes for expansion
- Reduced management overhead

## Skills Demonstrated

- **Financial Operations**: Redesigned entire month-end close process
- **Inventory Management**: Implemented comprehensive inventory tracking system
- **Cost Analysis**: Developed food cost control methodologies
- **Process Optimization**: Created scalable financial processes
- **Cross-Functional Leadership**: Coordinated multiple locations toward common goals
