# Grandlux Nail Salon: Managing a 17-Person Commission Floor

## Operational Context

I managed a 17-technician commission-based nail salon in McKinney, Texas. The job: handle unpredictable walk-ins, keep cash reconciliations perfect, and stop bad reviews before they happen.

**Context:** I took this role while finishing my UT Dallas degree (2021-2023). It was deliberate downtime after 12 intense years at Sonic—but I still kept my standards high.

## Starting Point

- **17 commission-based technicians** with ego-driven, artist-mentality workforce
- **Unverified cash reconciliation** practices in high-volume cash environment
- **Reactive reputation management** — responding to complaints after departure
- **Variable-demand capacity** with unpredictable walk-in volume
- **Texas cosmetology compliance** requirements
- **Commission fairness disputes** — gross ticket splits, tip allocation

## Confirmed Scope (and Limits)

**Core Function:**
- Floor management and real-time capacity optimization
- End-of-day cash reconciliation and controls
- Texas cosmetology sanitation and health regulation compliance
- Reputation management through proactive service recovery
- Technician pay fairness monitoring

**Out of Scope:**
- Inventory management (not part of this role)
- Social media management (not part of this role)
- Scheduling (not part of this role)

**How I managed complex floor operations:**
Matched clients to technicians based on skill, availability, and service time—like hotel front desks or ER triage, but for manicures. Same problem: unpredictable demand, limited supply.

## The Methodology

### Phase 1: Cash Control Implementation
- Standardized end-of-day reconciliation procedures
- Commission tracking verification
- Gross ticket split validation
- Tip allocation documentation

### Phase 2: Proactive Service Recovery
- Stayed on the floor, watched for unhappy body language
- Intercepted dissatisfied clients before they left
- Fixed issues in real-time
- Prevented bad Google reviews before they happened

### Phase 3: Compliance Maintenance
- Texas cosmetology regulation adherence
- Sanitation protocol enforcement
- Documentation maintenance
- Health inspection readiness

## The Outcome

### Quantitative Results
- **Cash Discrepancies**: Zero across every end-of-day reconciliation (third time in my career I've hit perfect reconciliation)
- **Google Review Complaints**: Volume reduced through proactive service recovery
- **Compliance**: Texas cosmetology sanitation and health regulation maintained throughout tenure

### Qualitative Impact
- Real-time capacity optimization achieved
- Technician commission fairness maintained
- Zero cash discrepancies in commission-based environment
- Proactive reputation management vs. reactive
- Showed I don't drop standards, even when the pressure's off
