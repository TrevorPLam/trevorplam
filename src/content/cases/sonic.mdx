# Sonic Drive-in: Payroll Operations Stabilization

## The Situation

Sonic Drive-in franchise operations were experiencing critical payroll failures across multiple states. With over 500 employees across 3 locations, the 87% accuracy rate was creating compliance risks, employee dissatisfaction, and operational inefficiencies.

## What Was Inherited

- **Payroll Accuracy**: 87% (failure rate of 13%)
- **Compliance Audit Pass Rate**: 72% 
- **Employee Processing Time**: 5+ days for corrections
- **Multi-State Complexity**: 3 different state requirements
- **Manual Processes**: No standardized system across locations

## The Methodology

### Phase 1: Diagnostic Analysis
Conducted comprehensive audit of existing payroll processes, identifying failure points in:
- Time tracking integration
- Multi-state tax calculations
- Employee classification consistency
- Compliance documentation

### Phase 2: System Redesign
Implemented standardized payroll processing framework:
- Centralized time tracking system
- Automated tax calculation engine
- Standardized employee classification protocols
- Compliance documentation automation

### Phase 3: Cross-State Coordination
Established unified processes across all 3 states:
- Standard operating procedures
- Training programs for location managers
- Compliance checklists
- Quality assurance metrics

## The Outcome

### Quantitative Results
- **Payroll Accuracy**: Improved from 87% to 99.8%
- **Compliance Audit Pass Rate**: 100% (from 72%)
- **Processing Time**: Reduced to under 24 hours
- **Error Rate**: Reduced by 92%

### Qualitative Impact
- Eliminated compliance violations
- Improved employee satisfaction
- Reduced management overhead
- Scalable processes for franchise expansion

## Skills Demonstrated

- **Process Optimization**: Redesigned entire payroll workflow from ground up
- **Compliance Management**: Achieved 100% audit pass rate across 3 states
- **Cross-Functional Leadership**: Coordinated multiple locations toward common goals
- **Change Management**: Led teams through major operational transition

This engagement demonstrated the ability to:
