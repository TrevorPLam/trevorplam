# Sonic Drive-in (D.L. Rogers Group): Revitalizing Unit Performance

## Operational Context

In 2016, I assumed leadership of a Sonic Drive-in unit with significant untapped potential. Within 18 months, the unit's performance had been completely revitalized, moving from bottom-tier to top 10% in company KPIs.

**My path over 12 years:** Carhop → Crew Leader/Traveling Trainer → Assistant Manager → Co-Manager/AMLC Instructor → Operating Partner/General Manager (2016-2020)

## Starting Point (2016)

- **Revenue**: $1.4M at unit with untapped potential
- **Food Cost Variance**: 2.7% (unsustainable)
- **Labor Cost Variance**: 3.9%
- **Employee Turnover**: 175%+ (industry average: 130-150%)
- **KPI Performance**: Bottom tier
- **Up to 40 employees** under management responsibility

## The Revitalization Methodology

**Ground-level observation** → **Leading by example** → **Standards reinforcement** → **Deliberate personnel triage prioritized by:**
1. Guest impact first
2. Culture second
3. Procedure third

### Phase 1: Food Cost System Redesign
Implemented education-first approach with daily KPI conversations:
- Systematic waste log analysis
- Portion control enforcement
- Spoilage tracking
- Theft pattern identification
- Accuracy standards reinforcement
- Performance-based reward (free meals) tied directly to improvement

**Result**: Food cost variance reduced from 2.7% to 0.75%

### Phase 2: Workforce Optimization
I built elastic staffing models myself—didn't delegate this:
- Disciplined scheduling
- Demand forecasting
- Pre-quit behavioral signal identification
- Proactive retention interventions

**Result**: Employee turnover reduced from 175%+ to under 80% — approximately 100 percentage point reduction

### Phase 3: Multi-Site Influence (Traveling Trainer)
Certified operational standards across **7 locations** with:
- Formal pass/fail assessment authority
- Gap analysis and corrective planning
- Influence without direct authority

### Phase 4: Organizational Recognition (AMLC Instructor)
I was selected as the **youngest and first Co-Manager in company history** to become an AMLC Instructor:
- Delivered formal management curriculum to classes of 30-50 students
- "Talent recognized from above" — not just curriculum delivery

## The Outcome

### Quantitative Results
- **Revenue**: $1.4M → $1.7M (~21% growth)
- **Sales**: 10%+ over budget
- **Profit**: 5%+ over budget
- **Food Cost Variance**: 2.7% → 0.75%
- **Labor Cost Variance**: 3.9% → -0.25%
- **Employee Turnover**: 175%+ → under 80%
- **KPI Performance**: Top 10% of company

### Recognition: Three Consecutive Annual Awards
- **Rookie of the Year (2016)**
- **Revitalization Operator of the Year (2017)**
- **Culture of Respect Award (2018)**

### The COVID Exit — Strategic Intelligence Signal
I left in November 2020 at my peak:
- Top 10% KPIs company-wide
- Three consecutive awards
- Youngest AMLC Instructor in organizational history
- **Eight months into COVID**, when restaurant industry faced most severe structural contraction in modern history
- **Declined Dallas partnership offer** specifically to pursue university degree

I read the room correctly—the restaurant industry was about to crater. Leaving at the top wasn't failure; it was knowing when to exit. That's the judgment you want in operations leadership.

## Confirmed Additional Scope

- Bank deposits and petty cash/change fund controls (zero-discrepancy)
- Safety and incident documentation (workers' comp, OSHA-adjacent)
- Equipment and facilities vendor management
- Staff scheduling (built personally, not delegated)
- Inventory ordering and management (direct daily practice)
- Full ownership: payroll, recruiting, interviewing, onboarding, performance management, vendor relations, billing, local store marketing, community outreach, food safety compliance

## Skills Demonstrated

- **P&L Management**: Full ownership of $1.4M→$1.7M unit revitalization
- **Operational Revitalization**: Transforming underperforming unit to top 10% performer
- **People Operations**: 100 percentage point turnover reduction
- **Multi-Site Training**: 7-location certification with pass/fail authority
- **Curriculum Development**: AMLC Instructor (youngest in org history)
- **Cost Control**: Food cost 2.7%→0.75%, labor cost 3.9%→-0.25%

## What This Role Proved

Over 12 years, I showed I can:
- Drive operational revitalization and performance improvement
- Earn three consecutive awards across different evaluation dimensions
- Achieve youngest-in-history organizational recognition
- Exit at peak performance rather than ride decline
- Apply same promotion mechanism at every level: finish work ahead of pace, explicitly request more responsibility, earn advancement based on demonstrated quality
- Build zero-discrepancy financial controls (daily deposits, petty cash, change funds)

**These aren't lucky breaks. It's the same pattern—arrive, diagnose, optimize, repeat—across three industries where I had zero prior technical knowledge.**
