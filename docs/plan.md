## What the Site Does NOT Include

Consistent with best practices for 2026: [cvireland](https://www.cvireland.com/blog/personal-website-mistakes-to-avoid/)

- No blog (unless you will publish at least monthly — a dead blog actively damages credibility)
- No skills progress bars ("90% proficient in Excel" means nothing and looks amateur)
- No photo gallery, personal social links, or anything unrelated to the work
- No dense walls of text — every section is scannable in under 15 seconds
- No auto-play anything — no video backgrounds, no music, no animations that can't be paused
