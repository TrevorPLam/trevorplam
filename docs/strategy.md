# Site Strategy

This document defines the primary conversion goal, audience personas, and visitor journeys for trevor-lam.com.

## Primary Conversion Goal

The primary conversion goal is **contact form submission** via the `/connect` page. This represents a qualified lead from a recruiter, founder, or operator interested in Trevor Lam's operations leadership, Chief of Staff, or HR/payroll expertise.

Secondary conversions include:
- LinkedIn profile visits (from footer and contact page)
- Resume PDF downloads (from about and connect pages)
- Case study PDF downloads (from individual case study pages)

## Audience Personas

### Recruiter (External Talent Acquisition)

**Demographics:**
- Role: Technical recruiter, executive search consultant, HR business partner
- Industry: Technology, operations, professional services
- Company size: Mid-market to enterprise (50+ employees)

**Psychographics:**
- Prioritizes quantified results and measurable impact
- Skeptical of vague claims without evidence
- Time-constrained, needs quick verification of credentials
- Values clear career progression and stability

**Goals:**
- Find qualified candidates for operations/Chief of Staff roles
- Verify candidate claims through concrete evidence
- Assess cultural fit and leadership potential

**Pain Points:**
- Resumes with inflated achievements or unclear impact
- Difficulty verifying operational expertise from traditional CVs
- Candidates lacking tangible proof of P&L responsibility

**Preferred Journey:**
1. Arrive via LinkedIn referral or search
2. Scan homepage metrics grid for quick credibility check
3. Review case studies for depth of operational experience
4. Check capabilities for alignment with role requirements
5. Download resume or submit contact form

---

### Founder (Startup/SMB Leadership)

**Demographics:**
- Role: Founder, CEO, COO of early-stage or growth-stage company
- Industry: QSR, professional services, operations-heavy businesses
- Company size: 10-100 employees (scaling phase)

**Psychographics:**
- Hands-on, pragmatic decision-maker
- Values operational stability over theoretical frameworks
- Seeks proven systems that can be implemented immediately
- Risk-averse regarding operational hires (expensive mistakes)

**Goals:**
- Stabilize chaotic operations
- Implement scalable systems and processes
- Find someone who can "fix it" without hand-holding

**Pain Points:**
- Operational chaos affecting growth
- Difficulty finding operators with real P&L experience
- Previous hires who talked strategy but couldn't execute

**Preferred Journey:**
1. Arrive via referral or search for "operations consultant"
2. Review case studies for similar industry challenges
3. Check capabilities for specific operational expertise
4. Download relevant playbooks/templates from resources
5. Submit contact form with specific operational challenge

---

### Operator (Peer/Network)

**Demographics:**
- Role: Operations manager, Director of Ops, VP of Operations
- Industry: QSR, professional services, logistics
- Company size: Any stage (peer learning)

**Psychographics:**
- Values practical, battle-tested approaches
- Interested in methodology and systems thinking
- Seeks community and knowledge exchange
- Appreciates transparency about failures and learning

**Goals:**
- Learn operational best practices
- Find templates and playbooks for immediate use
- Understand Trevor's approach to common operational challenges
- Network with other operations professionals

**Pain Points:**
- Generic operational advice that doesn't apply to their context
- Lack of concrete, implementable frameworks
- Isolation in operations role (no peer network)

**Preferred Journey:**
1. Arrive via social media or professional network
2. Explore learning log for methodology and thought leadership
3. Review resources for downloadable templates
4. Check projects for current experimental work
5. Engage via LinkedIn or contact form for collaboration

---

### Peer (Cross-Functional Professional)

**Demographics:**
- Role: HR leader, finance professional, technical manager
- Industry: Any industry with operational components
- Company size: Any stage

**Psychographics:**
- Curious about operations as a career path
- Values interdisciplinary learning
- Seeks to understand operational impact on their domain
- Appreciates clear communication of complex concepts

**Goals:**
- Understand operations leadership perspective
- Learn how operations intersects with their function
- Assess Trevor as a potential collaborator or reference

**Pain Points:**
- Operational jargon and unclear terminology
- Difficulty seeing relevance to their role
- Lack of accessible entry points to operational thinking

**Preferred Journey:**
1. Arrive via professional network or content sharing
2. Review about page for background and approach
3. Explore capabilities for high-level understanding
4. Check learning log for accessible explanations
5. Connect via LinkedIn for ongoing relationship

## Visitor Journeys

### Journey 1: Recruiter Fast-Track Verification

**Entry Points:**
- Direct URL (from resume or email signature)
- LinkedIn profile link
- Search for "Trevor Lam operations"

**Path:**
1. Homepage → Scan metrics grid (9 quantified achievements)
2. Homepage → Click "Case Studies" in navigation
3. Case Studies index → Review 3 industry summaries (QSR, Salon, CPA-Payroll)
4. Individual case study → Read "Initial State → What I Delivered → Outcome" structure
5. Case study → Download PDF for offline review
6. Navigation → Click "Capabilities" for skill alignment
7. Navigation → Click "Connect" → Submit form with job details

**Success Metrics:**
- Time to first case study: <30 seconds
- Case study PDF download rate
- Contact form submission with job details

---

### Journey 2: Founder Problem-Solving

**Entry Points:**
- Search for "operations turnaround consultant"
- Referral from peer network
- Content sharing (playbook/template download)

**Path:**
1. Homepage → Quick scan of positioning ("Operations Integrator · Forensic Stabilizer")
2. Navigation → Click "Case Studies"
3. Case Studies index → Identify relevant industry (e.g., QSR for restaurant founder)
4. Individual case study → Read full narrative for methodology
5. Case study → Click related capabilities links
6. Navigation → Click "Resources" → Download relevant playbooks
7. Resources → Review skills matrix for capability depth
8. Navigation → Click "Connect" → Submit form with specific challenge

**Success Metrics:**
- Resource download rate
- Time from homepage to resource download
- Contact form submission with operational context

---

### Journey 3: Operator Knowledge Discovery

**Entry Points:**
- Social media (LinkedIn, Twitter)
- Professional network referral
- Search for operational methodology

**Path:**
1. Homepage → Navigate to "Lab"
2. Lab → Click "Learning Log"
3. Learning Log index → Scan 6 entries for relevant topics
4. Individual learning log → Read methodology and insights
5. Learning log → Click relevant tags to find related content
6. Navigation → Click "Resources" → Download templates
7. Navigation → Click "Projects" → Review current experimental work
8. Footer → Click LinkedIn for connection

**Success Metrics:**
- Learning log engagement (time on page)
- Resource download rate
- LinkedIn profile visits

---

### Journey 4: Peer Cross-Functional Learning

**Entry Points:**
- Professional network referral
- Content sharing from peer
- Search for "HR operations" or "finance operations"

**Path:**
1. Homepage → Click "About" for background
2. About → Read career approach and value proposition
3. Navigation → Click "Capabilities"
4. Capabilities index → Review 5 capability categories
5. Individual capability → Read philosophy and KPIs
6. Capability → Click related case studies for proof
7. Navigation → Click "Lab" → Review learning log for accessible explanations
8. Footer → Connect via LinkedIn

**Success Metrics:**
- Time on capability pages
- Cross-navigation between capabilities and case studies
- LinkedIn connection rate

## Information Architecture Alignment

The site structure supports these journeys through:

**Primary Proof (Above the Fold):**
- Homepage metrics grid (9 quantified achievements)
- Direct case study access from navigation
- Clear positioning statement

**Supporting Evidence (Secondary Navigation):**
- Capabilities organized by functional area
- Resources for immediate practical application
- Learning log for methodology and thought leadership

**Trust Signals:**
- PDF downloads for offline review
- LinkedIn integration throughout
- Consistent quantified metrics across all content

**Conversion Points:**
- Contact form on dedicated page
- Resume download from about/connect
- LinkedIn links in footer and contact page
